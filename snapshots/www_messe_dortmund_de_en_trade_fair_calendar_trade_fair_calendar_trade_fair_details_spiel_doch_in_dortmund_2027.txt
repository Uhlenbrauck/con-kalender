Public fair for board and card games
SPIEL DOCH! in Dortmund invites you to discover, try out, and buy the latest innovations and popular classics from the world of board and card games in a relaxed atmosphere. This is the perfect place for you, especially if you are traveling with your family or are just getting started with this hobby.
A special highlight: on the large playing area, you can borrow games free of charge, have everything explained to you at your leisure, and start playing right away—whether alone, with friends, or with new players you meet on site.
